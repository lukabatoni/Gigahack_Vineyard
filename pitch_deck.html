Business case
The problem

Vineyards are inspected by hand — slowly, and at scale it doesn't work

A single site here is ~145 ha of planting, delivered as 311 tiles at 2.5 cm/px. A person walking that to map every plant, row, gap and piece of waste is days of work — and it's stale the moment it's done.

  • Government needs consistent, auditable block & row records across a region.
  • Farmers need to know where the problems are and the shortest way to reach them.
  • Manual annotation doesn't scale and isn't repeatable between inspectors.
User stories
  1. Government inspector — "Show me every block & row with consistent IDs, flag missing plantings, and give me one route that visits them all and returns to start."
  2. Farmer / owner — "Give me the shortest walk to collect waste and check disrupted rows, so I spend minutes in the field, not a day."

The solution

One pipeline: imagery in → decisions out

A reproducible, code-driven pipeline that produces the exact scored deliverables — CVAT annotations, per-block measurements, and a validated walking route — plus an interactive web map for two user roles.

Rows

Trained YOLO

Fine-tuned YOLO11-seg detects vine rows, stitched into consistent row IDs across tile edges.

Canopy

Per-plant CV

ExG vegetation index + watershed splits touching plants into individual canopy polygons.

Inter-row

Derived geometry

Walkable strips between adjacent rows, minus canopy — cover class from green fraction.

Route

Graph + TSP

Grid nav-graph over walkable ground, nearest-neighbour + 2-opt, returns to start.

Architecture · what runs inside

ML where it earns its keep, deterministic CV where it's reliable

Neural network

Row detection — YOLO11-seg

Single-class row model, fine-tuned on the exact-domain example tiles + hand-picked negative tiles (forest, field, village) to kill false positives. Tiled 640-px inference composited back to full 2048 tiles.

Classical CV

Canopy — ExG + watershed

Excess-Green index + adaptive threshold isolates foliage; distance-transform watershed separates touching plants into one-polygon-per-vine, per the golden rules.

Geometry / binary logic

Global IDs — world-space stitching

Rows reprojected to EPSG:32635; union-find groups blocks (<5 m gap) and stitches collinear row segments across tiles into stable vineyard_id / row_id.

Optimization

Route — NetworkX + 2-opt TSP

2 m nav-grid clipped to passages ∪ inter-row; Dijkstra between targets, then nearest-neighbour + 2-opt. Hard-gated: 100% inside walkable, closed loop.
